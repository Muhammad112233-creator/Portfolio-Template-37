0xMASON — Portfolio Template
-----------------------------

A human-made, responsive portfolio template built to mirror the look and feel of an award-nominated developer portfolio.

Tech: HTML5 · CSS (custom properties / SCSS mindset) · GSAP + ScrollTrigger
Fonts: Archivo · JetBrains Mono (via Google Fonts)

Built with care, not generated by AI.
Edit index.html, css/style.css and js/main.js to make it your own.
